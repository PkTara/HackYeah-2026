# Open Task: ImpactHer — Technology for Real Change

**Prize:** 8,000 PLN. Shared rules, criteria and AI policy: [open-tasks-common.md](open-tasks-common.md).

## Description

Technology creates meaningful change when it responds to real needs. Challenges around **safety, health, education, professional development, access to services, and participation in the digital world** can have a significant impact on women's lives. Some of these challenges are immediately visible; others only become clear with a closer understanding of everyday experiences and barriers.

**Task:** develop a technology-driven solution that addresses a **real challenge affecting women** and has the potential to create positive change. Any field, technology or approach is allowed; you choose the problem.

## Guidance

- Start with a **well-identified need** and show how your idea responds to it.
- Consider how the solution could **remove barriers, improve accessibility, expand opportunities, or make everyday life easier**.
- The judges want relevant, thoughtful ideas with practical potential.
- Show the value, how the solution would be used in real life, and the positive impact it aims for.

## Judging

Idea & Innovation 30% · Relation to Category 20% · Practical Applicability/Usability 20% · Design 20% · Completeness & Implementation Value 10%.
