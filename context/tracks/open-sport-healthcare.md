## Description

Everyday decisions about health and physical activity involve information that can be difficult to connect: training records, habits, wellbeing observations and healthcare information. The challenge is to turn these separate pieces into useful support.

**Task:** create a tool, application, system or prototype that helps people take an informed, active role in their health, physical activity or wellbeing. Choose a **specific user group** and **concrete need**, and make a meaningful activity or decision easier.

Possible directions:

- Understand patterns in activity, recovery and everyday routines.
- Support sustainable exercise habits adapted to different abilities and circumstances.
- Improve preparation for healthcare appointments and communication with professionals.
- Organize health information and share relevant details with caregivers.
- Improve access to physical activity and wellbeing support.

These examples are not mandatory or exhaustive. Users may also be families, sports communities, healthcare professionals or organizations.

## What to show the judges

- A clear user journey and the point where it provides practical value.
- How users understand their situation and take an **achievable next step**.
- Accessibility, motivation and the effort required for regular use.
- A specific need, rather than a broad collection of sports/health features.

## Submission and competition

- Team of 1–6; required: title, team name, members, description and PDF presentation of at most 10 slides.
- The track permits Polish or English. Snapshots, code repository, demo links, graphics and related materials are optional in the track PDFs.
- Also follow the [general upload constraints](../ProjectSubmissionUpload.md): English fields, title of at most five words, description of at most 500 words and required image gallery. Prepare to the narrower upload limits.

- Two phases: platform assessment by at least three mentors, followed by a live finalist pitch. At least 50% of phase-one points is required for an award, not a guarantee of one.
- Late revisions are not considered. Rules exclude participants related to jury members and employees of the prize-promising sponsor.
- Authors retain proprietary copyright. Prizes are issued within 90 days; jury names are due on Discord by 4 October.

## Existing resources and AI

Existing repositories and materials are allowed with citation and appropriate licensing. Disclose significant AI tools and external models, APIs, datasets and libraries. Prompt history and percentages of AI-generated code are not required.

The team remains responsible for originality, functionality, security and legal/licensing compliance. Explain technical decisions and distinguish pre-existing components from hackathon work. Judges assess actual technical work and team understanding. Plagiarism, misrepresentation and concealing significant pre-existing work can result in disqualification.

## Interpretation for Climbing Monkey

Use this as the primary product brief: connect climbing assessments and records with wellbeing context, show an understandable profile and support one achievable action. Motivation, accessibility and low routine effort matter as much as collecting information.

The brief does not mandate diagnosis, AI inference, health integrations, a particular platform, a public repository, a recorded demo or a file named `AI_WORKFLOW.md`. A focused activity-support prototype can fit this open task without implementing every example direction.
