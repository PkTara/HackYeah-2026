# Open Task: Defence

**Prize:** 8,000 PLN. Shared rules, criteria and AI policy: [open-tasks-common.md](open-tasks-common.md).

## Description

Security depends on the ability to recognize threats, respond effectively, and keep essential operations running under pressure. A disrupted service, a manipulated message or a compromised account can affect an entire organization or community. How can technology help people prepare for these situations and make better decisions when they happen?

**Task:** develop a solution that **strengthens security and resilience**. Choose a specific problem, identify who faces it, and build a tool, application, system or prototype that helps **prevent threats, detect them earlier, or reduce their consequences**.

Possible directions (starting points only; other defence or security ideas are welcome):
- Helping smaller organizations identify weaknesses and prioritize protective measures.
- Supporting the verification of suspicious messages, content or information sources.
- Improving coordination and information sharing during emergencies.
- Mapping dependencies between services and identifying potential points of failure.
- Making security procedures easier to understand and follow.

## Guidance

- Focus on a **realistic scenario** and show how the solution supports the people involved.
- Consider what happens when **information is incomplete, resources are limited, or services become unavailable** (degraded or offline operation).
- A strong project makes its value clear through a demo of how it improves **preparedness, response or continuity**.

## Judging

Idea & Innovation 30% · Relation to Category 20% · Practical Applicability/Usability 20% · Design 20% · Completeness & Implementation Value 10%.
