# Open Task: Artificial Intelligence

**Prize:** 8,000 PLN. Shared rules, criteria and AI policy: [open-tasks-common.md](open-tasks-common.md).

## Description

AI creates new opportunities to work with information, produce content, learn and perform complex tasks. Its value depends on how it is applied and what it does for the people using it.

**Task:** create a solution in which **AI plays a meaningful role** and addresses a **specific user need**. You may use existing models, APIs and tools, combine them with your own components, or develop your own approach.

Possible directions:
- Making complex information easier to find and understand.
- Supporting learning and adapting how knowledge is presented.
- Improving the accessibility of content and services.
- Assisting creative work and the development of ideas.
- Automating selected stages of a workflow.
- Helping users analyze situations and compare possible actions.

Open to any industry or technology, and to practical or unconventional applications.

## What to show the judges

- The **role AI plays**, how the components work together, and what benefits they provide.
- Your **technical decisions**, plus the solution's **capabilities and limitations**.
- **How users can verify the outputs and stay in control** (human-in-the-loop, citations, confidence, override).
- A **concrete use case** that makes the value easy to understand.

## Judging

Idea & Innovation 30% · Relation to Category 20% · Practical Applicability/Usability 20% · Design 20% · Completeness & Implementation Value 10%.
